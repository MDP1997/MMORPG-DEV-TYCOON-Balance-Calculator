## 🇬🇧 English
**📖 Description**

This is a calculator to simulate and balance your character for the actual version in MMORPG DEV TYCOON developed by Elindery


**⚔️ Combat Simulation**

- Automatic 1v1 combat simulation
- AI system that selects the optimal skill based on:
- Estimated total damage
- Damage over time
- Current state (HP, Mana, buffs, debuffs)
- Cooldown, cost, and restriction handling (silence, disarm, blind, stun)
- Automatic HP and Mana regeneration every 5 seconds

**📊 Detailed & Readable Combat Logs**
Combat generates a chronological, color-coded log for easy analysis:
- ⚪ Direct damage
- 🔴 Light red: Damage over Time (DoT)
- 🟣 Critical hits
- 🟢 Healing & life leech
- 🔵 Cyan: Mana leech & mana regeneration
- 🔵 actions, stuns, evade/resist
- 🟡 Debuffs
- 🟠 Buffs
- ℹ️ States, dispels, costs, and informational events

Each log entry displays:

- Skill used
- Damage or effect applied
- HP/Mana before and after
- Active buffs and debuffs at that moment

**🧪 Class Balance & Analysis**

Results can be grouped by:

- Primary class
- Class combinations (e.g. Mage–Assassin)
- Secondary archetypes
  
Helps identify:
- Dominant builds
- Overpowered or underperforming classes
- Problematic skill synergies

The goal is to allow designing and balancing characters without the need to perform complex calculations.
